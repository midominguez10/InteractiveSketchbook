Reading Reflection 1

The only affordance from my micro project is the space bar. The way that this affordance is signified is the start screen, where it says “PRESS SPACE TO START” and it disappears when you press it. There are also some signifiers that show the differences among the three shapes. You obviously wouldn’t know how much time each would take but I feel that is part of the game. Something that is unclear may be the shapes reshuffling and speed increase which happen silently, maybe a small warning or notice that these things are happening would be fair to the player.

The feedback in my project is the timer that is counting down, as well as the win or loss message at the end of the game. One moment when my system fails to communicate is when the shape is selected, there is no indicator like a sound or flash or a text to show that I chose a shape. I feel like that is the main thing that Norman would criticize because that is the main point of the game of choosing the shape and there is nothing to show that you actually chose the shape.

I think the relationship between the shape lining up with the diamond is intuitive. I feel like in the beginning when the player first tries to play the game the player would not know which one is the best shape to choose or aim for. The only thing that the player might be able to understand is from the key because they can probably assume which one would be the fastest and the slowest shape to choose.

The constraints are that the spacebar only works and that there is only 3 shapes to choose from. These constraints are all invisible. The window of choosing the shape is also not shown so it might confuse the player, along with no signal to show that the shape was chosen.

If Norman evaluated my project, like I said before I think he would say my weakness in the design would be indicating when the shape was chosen, it is the most important part of the game and there is no way of seeing whether the shape was chosen or not. Also, when the shape is chosen, there is no way of communicating that the speed increased and that the shapes have been reshuffled. Maybe adding a flash like I said maybe a -15 seconds on the screen when the shape is chosen would be a great improvement to the project.

I feel like it relates because Norman talks about how the bad design influenced him to solve the problem in his own way like with the refrigerator so that can relate with the IxD at a macro level influencing human behavior. I like the reading because it makes you think about your design more, and how I want to add stuff to my own project to make it more understandable to the user.
